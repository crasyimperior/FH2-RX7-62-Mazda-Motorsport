**Mazda RX-7 Mazda Motorsport #62 (1991 IMSA GTO), ported from Forza Motorsport 6 to Forza Horizon 2 PC. This is the version with headlights.**

New car, car Id **19921**, sold at the Autoshow (Mazda, Hypercars) for **650,000 CR**.

- Full FM6 car: body, Mazda Motorsport livery, cockpit, gold mesh wheels, its own physics and upgrades.
- Its own engine sound (FM6 recordings, own sound bank).
- Taillights like in FM6 (night + brake glow).
- Night headlights added: clear glass covers with a lamp behind, lit with the game's headlights.

## Requirements
• [Azimuth](https://almost-there-games.itch.io/azimuth) 1.0.4
• [Extra Options](https://github.com/RokkuDayo/ForzaHorizon2_ExtraOptions) 0.4.4+ (loads the `mods` folder)

## Install
Close the game, extract `RX7_62_Mazda_Motorsport_v1.0.zip` into the `mods` folder of your Azimuth folder (you must get `mods\MAZ_62_RX7_91 Mazda Motorsport\mod.json`), start the game and buy the car at the Autoshow.

**FR :** ferme le jeu, extrais le zip dans le dossier `mods` de ton dossier Azimuth (tu dois obtenir `mods\MAZ_62_RX7_91 Mazda Motorsport\mod.json`), lance le jeu et achète la voiture au Salon auto (Mazda, 650 000 CR).

Note: the mod ships its own `MasterBank.strings.bank`; another mod replacing the same file will conflict.
